Hi! I’m Shravan Nayak. I am a PhD student in computer science at [Mila](https://mila.quebec/en/) and the [University of Montreal](https://www.umontreal.ca/en/) supervised by Prof. Aishwarya Agrawal. My research focuses on **building inclusive and robust multimodal systems**. Currently, my work spans three areas:

**Culture & Alignment.** I study how AI can recognize and adapt to diverse cultural perspectives. This includes benchmarking cultural understanding in VLMs ([CulturalVQA](https://culturalvqa.org/)) and text-to-image models ([CulturalFrames](https://culturalframes.github.io/)), studying pluralistic values and alignment in T2I models ([LIVS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.07683)), and tracing value alignment shifts during LLM post-training ([Value Drifts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26707)).

**Agents.** I created the [CUA-Suite](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24440) for computer-use agents, which includes the [UI-Vision](https://uivision.github.io/) benchmark, the [GroundCUA](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ServiceNow/GroundCUA) dataset (downloaded over 150k times), the [GroundNext](https://huggingface.co/ServiceNow/GroundNext-7B-V0) series of grounding models, and the [VideoCUA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24440) dataset. I am also interested in deploying agents for the enterprise ([EnterpriseOps-Gym](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13594)).

**Understanding & Improving VLMs.** I am curious about the inner workings of VLMs and how understanding them can help improve their robustness and reliability. I have worked on [adversarial robustness in VLMs](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-emnlp.990/), interpretablity of visual representations in LLMs ([LatentLens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00462)), and [discovering failure modes in VLMs using RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04733).

In my free time, I love to cook and share my experiences on my [blog](/blog/). I also have a deep interest in history, culture, and archaeology. I find it fascinating to explore the diverse ways of life and the events that have shaped our world.
